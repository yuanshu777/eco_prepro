EV9V cardiac-view coverage by filename-derived group
Frozen data: 2026-10-05 EDA inventory; analysis added 2026-10-06 UTC

Scope and method
All 5,138 original EV9V videos, using official clip-level labels.
Primary grouping: leading YYYY-MM-DD_HH-MM-SS, producing 495 groups.
Sensitivity grouping: literal prefix before _320x240, producing 685 groups.
The latter retains optional segment/numeric tokens and subdivides 92 primary
groups. Neither rule is validated as a patient or examination identifier.
No group crosses the official dataset splits, but this is not identity proof.
Coverage = groups with at least one clip carrying the label / all groups.
Repeated matching clips give the same group only one vote for that view.

Findings: primary date-time groups
Median: 7 videos and 4 distinct released labels per group.
32 groups have one observed label; 17/495 (3.4%) have all nine labels.
There are 153 exact label combinations. The most frequent is PLHLA + A4C
only, with 51/495 groups (10.3%). These are exact combinations, not pairs
that may also occur within larger combinations.

Merged PSAX coverage is 366/495 (73.9%). It is the union of groups containing
PASA, PMVLSA, PPMLSA or PMASA, not a sum of their individual percentages.
PLHLA is renamed PLAX. PMPALA remains a separate sixth reporting category.
All videos are retained under both raw-nine and merged-six label schemes.

Grouping sensitivity
The detailed-prefix rule gives a median of 6 videos and 4 raw labels per
group. It has 69 single-label groups and 13 groups with all nine labels.
Raw-view coverage decreases by 3.14 to 5.62 percentage points compared with
the date-time rule. PASA has the largest decrease, from 39.19% to 33.58%.
Each rule uses its own full group denominator; no low-coverage group is dropped.

Interpretation
A missing cell means no released clip label of that type was observed in
that filename group. It does not prove that the view was never acquired.
The official release reports 703 patients but supplies no usable video-to-
patient mapping. Groups must not be presented as patients or complete exams.
A2C is outside the EV9V label ontology, not a missing expected category.
Video-level labels do not establish frame-level or transition ground truth.
No patient-level confidence intervals or clinical completeness claims are made.

Reproduction
Copy the pre-coverage v5 EDA directory to a new output directory. The extension
checks the frozen inventory hash and refuses to overwrite existing coverage.
  .venv/bin/python scripts/eda_ev9v_coverage.py --out outputs/eda_new_revision
  .venv/bin/python scripts/eda_report.py --out outputs/eda_new_revision
  .venv/bin/python scripts/eda_explain.py --out outputs/eda_new_revision
The first command creates the tables, figures and standalone report; the
second integrates sections 4.12-4.17; the third updates the English guide.
No raw pixels, model inference, rescan or change to preprocessing is required.
