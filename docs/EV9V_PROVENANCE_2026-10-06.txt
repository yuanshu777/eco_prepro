EV9V long-video provenance audit
Audit date: 2026-10-06 UTC
EDA snapshot examined: 2026-10-05, report version v5

Finding
The long EV9V videos in the current EDA are already present in the official
published dataset. Local concatenated demo videos exist, but none are inputs
to this EDA. This conclusion is based on file contents, not filename patterns.

Verification
1. Pinned publisher revision: 094135b4da40c9aed9d9550c65305dc8715c7d3a.
2. Local Videos.tar is 6,606,477,312 bytes. Its full SHA-256 equals the publisher's
   LFS SHA-256 obtained from the public Hugging Face repository API:
   1d991e766949e35bc88b9fb07ff2485acffaa98ee7f956c10935c3a1765593ef
3. The official archive contains 5,138 MP4 files with 5,138 unique video IDs.
   Those IDs exactly match both ev9v_native.csv and the EV9V EDA inventory.
4. SHA-256 was computed independently for every local EDA MP4 and its member
   inside Videos.tar: 5,138/5,138 match, with zero content mismatches.
5. Git blob hashes of the three original split lists and README.md also equal
   the publisher's objects at the pinned revision.
6. scripts/eda_inventory.py reads data/manifests/ev9v_native.csv in the companion
   echo-view-routing project. It does not read constructed-stream recipe banks
   or runs/_demo/streams. None of the 33 MP4s in that demo directory is an EDA
   input. The current EDA therefore needs no merged-video exclusion.

All thresholds above are strict greater-than comparisons.
The publisher's dataset card independently gives a maximum of 3,243 frames
and a rate of 30 fps, which implies 108.1 s of playback.
Section 4.3 currently displays 0-80 s for EV9V and annotates the one video
outside that range. The plot limit is not the dataset maximum; the outlier
remains in the inventory and summary statistics.

Concatenation artifacts located in the companion project
Directory: /home/william/echo-view-routing/runs/_demo/streams/
Current provenance index: streams.json and recipes.json.
There are 33 rendered MP4s, of which 27 are in the current index. Among those
27, eight use one source fragment and 19 concatenate multiple fragments:
five same-view joins, three same-view joins with a gamma edit, eight
cross-view joins, and three streams containing four to six fragments.
Six additional MP4s lack entries in the current index; their fragment
provenance is not inferred from their names.

Examples (durations also checked in the MP4 headers):
  multi_00.mp4: 4 fragments,  9.47 s; A4C -> A4C -> PLAX -> PSAX.
  multi_01.mp4: 5 fragments, 12.50 s; PSAX -> A5C -> A4C -> SC4C -> PLAX.
  multi_02.mp4: 6 fragments, 14.33 s; PLAX -> PLAX -> A4C -> A5C -> A4C -> PSAX.
  diff_PLAX_A4C.mp4: 2 fragments, 6.0 s; PLAX -> A4C.
The streams index records nominal durations of 9.5, 12.5 and 14.5 s for these
three multi-fragment examples. Actual rendered durations above are from MP4
headers, not nominal fragment counts. These two time bases are kept separate.
Each indexed recipe identifies its source video IDs and fragment offsets.
scripts/make_demo_streams.py concatenates source frames and renders MP4s.
echo_routing/compose/stream_assembler.py separately concatenates cached
features/probabilities according to recipes. A recipe is not an additional
original recording or necessarily a rendered video.
There are 18 recipe-bank JSON files in data/manifests/recipes/, including
multiple versions. One multi-fragment bank version has 1,500 training,
120 validation and 120 test recipes; these counts are not added to the EDA.

Limits of the conclusion
Byte identity establishes equivalence to the published EV9V files. It cannot
establish whether the dataset authors edited recordings before publication,
or prove that each video represents a continuous acquisition or one view
throughout. The local artifacts do not establish who created them; attribution
to the user's friend is unverified. No videos, model outputs or EDA figures
were changed by this investigation.
